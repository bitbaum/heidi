# Roadmap

Heidi makes Zurich German understandable — situation by situation, for individuals and for organisations whose people live and work here. This is the order we are building it in, and why. Nothing here is dated: the order carries the argument, and nothing is presented as shipped before it is.

This file is the copy the fleet map (`loki.orangecat.ch/api/fleet/map`) reads. The bilingual source the site renders from is `lib/config/roadmap.ts`; the two change in the same PR, and a test keeps their items equal.
